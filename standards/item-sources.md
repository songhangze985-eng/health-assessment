# 按条目的原文与国际标准

原话只来自这次打开的页面，或本仓库先前已经打开并核对过的页面。英文放在引号里。中文只说明能编码的范围。没有打开到可引用原句的条目写「未引用」，不用邻近标准冒充。

技能仍不下诊断、不开药。下面的数字是人群标准或计分对照，不是处方。

## 必填

### 蛋白质

本次沿用已打开的 ISSN 2017 立场，未重新打开全文。

> “For building muscle mass and for maintaining muscle mass through a positive muscle protein balance, an overall daily protein intake in the range of 1.4–2.0 g protein/kg body weight/day (g/kg/d) is sufficient for most exercising individuals.”

Jäger 等，Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition，2017。https://doi.org/10.1186/s12970-017-0177-8

可编码：运动者 1.4–2.0 克/公斤/天。默认 1.6 落在这段里。固定的 140 克或 150 克不在这段原话里。

世界卫生组织健康膳食事实页另有一句，对象是一般成人能量比例，不是运动增肌标准，不能替换上面这一条：

> “Protein intake at 10–15% of total daily energy intake is generally sufficient to meet the needs of adults (approximately 50–75 grams for a person of healthy body weight consuming about 2000 calories per day).”

https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet

### 抗阻

本次打开 Bull 等对世界卫生组织 2020 指南的说明，British Journal of Sports Medicine。https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7719906/

> “Adults should also do muscle-strengthening activities at moderate or greater intensity that involve all major muscle groups on 2 or more days a week, as these provide additional health benefits.”

可编码：每周至少 2 天，覆盖各大肌群。技能默认每周 3 次，是打卡对照，不是这句原话里的唯一次数。

### 超负荷

本次打开的是 ACSM 2011 立场文本的公开 PDF。Garber 等，Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise，2011。https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0b013e318213fefb

> “A gradual progression of greater resistance, and/or more repetitions per set, and/or increasing frequency is recommended.”

可编码：负荷、次数或频率可以逐步增加。这句没有说「不加码就没有训练效果」。

## 身体选填

### 睡眠时长

本次未重新打开共识全文。沿用先前打开的 AASM 与睡眠研究学会 2015 共识页。Watson 等，Sleep，2015。https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4434546/

> “Adults should sleep 7 or more hours per night on a regular basis to promote optimal health.”

可编码：18–60 岁，是否达到 7 小时。这句没有把超过 9 小时写成扣分上限。

### 结构均衡

本次打开世界卫生组织健康膳食事实页。https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet

> “A variety of minimally processed and unprocessed foods low in unhealthy fats, free sugars and sodium are the foundation of any healthy diet.”

> “Everyone older than 10 years of age should aim for at least 400 grams of fruits and vegetables per day.”

可编码：10 岁以上，水果加蔬菜是否达到每天 400 克。这是膳食结构的一条人群目标，不是一餐的配比公式。

### 饮食质量

同一页：

> “The consumption of free sugars should be limited to less than 10% of total daily energy intake, which is equivalent to 50 g (or about 12 level teaspoons) for a person of healthy body weight consuming about 2000 calories per day.”

> “In adults, salt intake should be limited to less than 5 grams per day (2 grams per day sodium).”

可编码：游离糖低于总能量 10%；成人食盐低于 5 克/天。进一步降到 5% 能量是「可能有额外好处」，不是第二条必达线。

### 总饮水量

本次打开 EFSA 2010 水的膳食参考值意见摘要。EFSA Journal。https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/1459

> “The reference values for total water intake include water from drinking water, beverages of all kind, and from food moisture and only apply to conditions of moderate environmental temperature and moderate physical activity levels (PAL 1.6).”

> “Available data for adults permit the definition of AIs as 2.0 L/day (P 95 3.1 L) for females and 2.5 L/day (P95 4.0 L) for males.”

可编码：适度温度、中等活动量下，总水（含水和饮料，也含食物中的水）女性 2.0 升/天、男性 2.5 升/天。这不是「只喝白水 2 升」。

### 饮品结构

世界卫生组织口腔卫生事实页，2025-03-17。https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/oral-health

> “promoting a well-balanced diet low in free sugars and high in fruit and vegetables, and favouring water as the main drink”

可编码：把水当作主要饮品，并限制游离糖。没有打开到咖啡、茶、酒精各自的升数上限。

### 有氧运动

Bull 等，2020，同上 PMC。

> “Adults should do at least 150–300 min of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity, or at least 75–150 min of vigorous-intensity aerobic physical activity, or an equivalent combination of moderate-intensity and vigorous-intensity activity throughout the week for substantial health benefits.”

> “Aerobic physical activity: Activity in which the body’s large muscles move in a rhythmic manner for a sustained period of time.”

可编码：每周至少 150 分钟中等强度，或至少 75 分钟较大强度，或二者组合。150–300 与 75–150 是「有实质性好处」的范围，不是超过就扣分。

### 柔韧拉伸

ACSM 2011 公开 PDF，同上。

> “A series of flexibility exercises for each of the major muscle–tendon units is recommended.”

> “A reasonable target is to perform 60 s of total stretching time for each flexibility exercise.”

可编码：各大肌腱单元都做拉伸，每个动作总拉伸时间 60 秒是一个合理目标。PDF 文本层把「每周至少 2–3 天」的符号拆乱了，这次不把那一行当成干净原话。

同一份世界卫生组织 2020 说明里，孕妇和产后有一句 “Adding gentle stretching may also be beneficial.” 这是该人群的补充说明，不拿来当全体成人的柔韧标准。

### 久坐时长

Bull 等，2020，同上。

> “Sedentary behaviour: Any waking behaviour characterised by an energy expenditure of 1.5 METs or lower while sitting, reclining or lying.”

> “Additionally, reducing sedentary behaviours is recommended across all age groups and abilities, although evidence was insufficient to quantify a sedentary behaviour threshold.”

可编码：久坐是清醒时坐、靠或躺、能量消耗不超过 1.5 MET。减少久坐被建议，但没有一条可打分的小时上限。

### 黄毛：评价分类

沿用先前打开的 Kluger 与 DeNisi，1996，Psychological Bulletin，119(2):254–284。https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.119.2.254

已核对的机制是：反馈把注意力从任务拉向自我时，效果变弱；平均上反馈有帮助，同时超过三分之一的反馈干预降低了表现。这篇没有「留下可测量评价、丢弃人身攻击」的原句。该分类仍是技能规则。

## 计分时怎么用

能编码的条目，用户写下数字时，用上面的范围做对照，写进依据。没有打开到切点的条目，仍只接受用户自己的 1–5，不换成一个编造的国际分数。必填的 80/20 和不满 7 天不出总分，仍按 [scoring.md](scoring.md)。
